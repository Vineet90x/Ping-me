# Salon Owner Validation — Field Kit

**Goal:** find out if neighborhood salon owners have a pain worth paying for — *before* building more.

**The one decision:** Talk to **8–10 owners**. If **3+** would genuinely pay (real commitment, see scorecard) → build. If not → drop it and move on. You'll have spent two weekends, not two months.

---

## The 5 rules (read before you go)
1. **Don't pitch your app.** The moment you describe the product, they'll be polite and lie. Talk about *their* life, not your idea.
2. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "How many no-shows last week?" beats "Would you use a reminder app?" People are honest about what they *did*, fantasists about what they *would* do.
3. **Compliments are worthless.** "Nice idea, beta!" = a no. Ignore it.
4. **Dig into money and pain.** "That sounds annoying — what does it actually cost you?" Real pain has a rupee number attached.
5. **Shut up and listen.** You're collecting truth, not closing a sale. Let silences hang.

---

## The conversation (speak in Hindi/Marathi — script is Hinglish)

**Warm-up (don't mention any app)**
- "Aapka salon kitne saal se chal raha hai? Kitne log kaam karte hain — kitni chairs hain?"
- "Ek din mein kitne customer aate hain roughly?"

**No-show pain (the big one)**
- "Log appointment lete hain ya direct aa jaate hain?"
- "Pichle hafte kitne log aaye nahi jinhone bola tha aayenge?" *(listen for a number)*
- "Jab koi nahi aata, toh kya hota hai — chair khaali rehti hai?" → "Uska kitna nuksaan hua hoga mahine mein?" *(get a ₹ figure)*

**Empty-slot / win-back pain**
- "Subah ya weekday afternoon mein dukaan slow rehti hai? Tab kya karte ho?"
- "Jo purane customer 2-3 mahine se nahi aaye — unko wapas bulane ke liye kuch karte ho?" *(do they do anything at all? mostly "no" = opportunity)*

**Money / payment pain**
- "Payment kaise lete ho — cash, UPI?" → "Bill dete ho customer ko? Kaise?"
- "Mahine ke end mein hisaab kaise rakhte ho — kaun aaya, kitna aaya?"

**Current tools (what are you replacing?)**
- "Abhi booking ka record kaise rakhte ho — diary, WhatsApp, kuch app?"
- "Koi software try kiya tha pehle? (Kyun chhoda?)" *(if they tried & quit, find out why — that's your churn risk)*

**The money question (only near the end, watch the face)**
- "Maan lo ek cheez aapke no-shows aadhe kar de aur slow din bhar de — reminders aur purane customer wapas. Iske liye mahine ka ₹500 dena banta hai?"
- Then the real test: **"Main agle hafte aapke salon ke liye set karke laata hoon — aap try karoge?"** *(Asking for their time/commitment now is the truest signal. A real yes leans in; a fake yes says "haan haan dikhana kabhi.")*

**Only AFTER the questions:** show the bot on your phone for 60 seconds. Watch their reaction — do they grab your phone and try it, or nod politely? Reaction > words.

## Things to *observe* (don't ask — just notice)
- [ ] Is WhatsApp open on their counter phone all day? (your whole bet rests on this)
- [ ] How do they track today's bookings *right now* — diary, register, memory, nothing?
- [ ] Did the owner answer, or a busy receptionist? (who'd actually use it?)
- [ ] Did a real no-show / empty chair happen while you were standing there?

## Decision gate (be strict with yourself)

- **3+ salons score ≥7 AND said yes to "try it next week"** → **Build.** You have a wedge. Onboard those 3 free, watch them use it for 2 weeks, *then* talk pricing.
- **Pain is real but nobody will commit** → the problem isn't worth paying to solve *for them*. **Reposition or drop.**
- **Even the pain is weak (lots of 0s on no-shows/win-back)** → **Drop it.** Move to the next idea with a clear conscience — you validated instead of guessing.
